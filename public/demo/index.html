Every obligation owned, backed up, finished — with a receipt.

Remindify is one register for recurring business operations: vendor renewals, compliance filings, contractor deliverables and invoice collections. This walkthrough shows the live product — AI-first capture, the AG Grid register, agent sweeps through the time machine, and a PayPal authorization hold that releases only on verified proof.

Chapters

What you're looking at

  • 1

    One register, both sides of the operation. Work owed to a regulator or a contractor, and invoices that leave or arrive on a date. Every row has an owner and a named backup.

  • 2

    Autonomy is a ceiling, not a switch. An agent can prepare the work or execute it, per agent and per obligation. You approve anything above the ceiling, and the spend cap stays visible.

  • 3

    Capture is AI-first. Gemini 2.5 Flash returns structured obligations from a notice or an email; a deterministic parser takes over when no key is configured. You review every field before it is scheduled.

  • 4

    Proof plus escrow. Completed work files a signed receipt. The payer's funds sit on a PayPal authorization until the multimodal auditor passes the evidence — text, photo or scanned PDF — then capture and payout settle the net to the provider.

Honest status. The settlement path shown at 1:50 ran against the PayPal sandbox, read back from the PayPal API, so no live money moves. The product is live; external usage is still early.
