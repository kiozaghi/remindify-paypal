What Remindify does

  • 1

    One register, both sides of the operation. Track recurring obligations and invoices with an owner, a risk level and a named backup.

  • 2

    Autonomy has limits. Scoped agents can prepare or execute work within assigned permissions and spend ceilings; higher-risk actions stay with a human.

  • 3

    Capture turns documents into work. The app structures notices and emails into proposed obligations for review before scheduling.

  • 4

    Payments require evidence. PayPal Sandbox authorizations can be captured after evidence review; this prototype does not handle live-money settlement.

Honest status. The live app is a hackathon prototype. Payment and payout actions use PayPal Sandbox; the demo does not represent live settlement availability.
